Sparse autoencoders on the grokked modular-addition transformer

Libor "Burny" Burian · · code & paper · based on Nanda et al. 2023

A one-layer transformer learned (a + b) mod 113 by placing numbers on circles. We train sparse autoencoders (SAEs) on its activations, check every feature against the known algorithm, and steer features to change what the network computes. Everything below is computed in your browser from the real model and SAE weights.

Status

1 · The MLP input is a cone over a circle

For each key frequency k, attention writes a and b into the same 2-D plane: zk = eiw a + eiw b = 2cos(w(a−b)/2)·eiw(a+b)/2. The angle carries half the sum, so the colour (the sum's phase) cycles twice per turn.
